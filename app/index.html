Welcome

This prototype explores how AI explanations and verification prompts can support human decision-making in social media moderation.

Your task

Review social media comments and decide whether to KEEP or REMOVE them according to the moderation policy.

Important information

  • AI recommendations are simulated.
  • AI confidence levels are simulated.
  • AI recommendations may be incorrect.
  • You retain the final decision.
  • No real content will be removed.
